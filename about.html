About this glossary

The UJ Multilingual Engineering Glossary is a working glossary of assessment and instruction terminology used in the Faculty of Engineering and the Built Environment at the University of Johannesburg. It gives each English term alongside its isiZulu, Afrikaans and Sesotho sa Leboa equivalents, so that students can look up the words examiners and lecturers use to phrase questions and instructions.

The current 139 entries were extracted from four study guides: the 2025 CAD Learner Guide, the Introduction to Engineering Design 1A Study Guide (2026), the Engineering Drawing 1A (EDRMIA) Study Guide (2025), and the Graphical Communication GKM EEA1 Study Guide (2026). They cover the assessment “action words” used in questions (Apply, Calculate, Compare, Explain, and so on), the six levels of Bloom’s Taxonomy verbs, key academic policy terms such as Plagiarism, Copyright and Fair Dealing, and core engineering-drawing/CAD terms such as Orthographic Projection, Sectional View and Datum.

The isiZulu, Afrikaans and Sesotho sa Leboa entries are an AI-assisted first-pass draft, produced to give the project a working multilingual base to build on. They have not yet been checked by a terminologist and should be reviewed and corrected before they are treated as final or published to students.
